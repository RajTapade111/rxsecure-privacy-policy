1. Introduction

RxSecure (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a hospital billing and prescription management application designed to securely handle patient data, medical charges, lab billing, and prescription workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Patient name, age, gender
  • Contact details
  • Prescription details
  • Billing and payment information
  • Hospital/clinic identifiers
  • User login details (if applicable)

2.2 Non‑Personal Information

  • Device information
  • App usage analytics
  • Crash logs
  • Technical performance data

2.3 Sensitive Medical Information

RxSecure handles lab reports, medical charges, IPD/OPD billing, and prescription data strictly for medical and billing purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate accurate billing
  • Manage lab and medical charges
  • Create and store prescriptions
  • Improve app performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain audit‑ready medical records
  • Enable secure login and role‑based access

4. Data Storage & Security

We use industry‑standard security practices including encrypted communication (HTTPS), secure Firestore database rules, role‑based access control, and token‑based authentication.

5. Third‑Party Services

RxSecure may use:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Razorpay (for payment processing)
  • Google Play Services

6. Login & Authentication

If your app requires login, user credentials are securely stored, passwords are never stored in plain text, and access is restricted based on user roles.

7. Payment Information

If Razorpay is used, payment details are processed securely. We do not store card or UPI information. Razorpay’s privacy policy applies for payment processing.

8. Data Sharing

We may share data only with authorized hospital staff, to comply with legal requirements, or for audit and billing verification. We never share data with advertisers.

9. Your Rights

10. Children’s Privacy

RxSecure is not intended for children under 13 unless used in a medical context by authorized staff.
